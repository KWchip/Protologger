What is Protologger?

Protologger is an all-in-one genome description tool, aimed at simplifying the process of gathering the data required for writing protologues. This includes providing taxonomic, functional and ecological insights, described in detail below:
Taxonomic placement
- 16S rRNA gene sequence identity values
- 16S rRNA gene-based phylogenetic tree
- Taxonomic assignment based on GTDB (GTDB-Tk r89)
- Genomic tree (phylophlan3)
- ANI values (FastANI)
- POCP values
Functional analysis
- KEGG based pathway reconstruction
- CAZyme profiling
- Antibiotic resistance profiling (CARD)
Ecological analysis
- Prevalence and average relative abundance across 1,000 samples for 19 unique environments (IMNGS)
- Occurrence based on MASH comparison to a database of >50,000 MAGs from thousands of samples

Instructions

The video below described how to use Protologger;


The major steps for submission are also described below;
- Registering an account (GDPR mode has been activate in compliance with European law) will provide you with 1Gb of space, suitable for multiple analysis.

- For the sake of simplicity, we suggest you next make individualised 'Histories' for each isolate you run through Protologger. To do this click on the '+' on the left next to 'History'. You can then name the history based on the isolate you are currently studying by clicking on 'Unnamed history' and editing it as needed.

- The first thing you need to do in your isolate specific history is upload your data. To do this you can click 'Get data' on the tool bar to the right and upload your genome and 16S rRNA sequence from your PC. Both files should be in FASTA format and only contain valid base pairs. Sequences in Excel, Word or any other format will not be recognised.

- Now you are ready to run Protologger so click on the 'Protologer' tab under the 'Tools' bar on the right and click again on Protologger. This will bring you to the input selection screen where you can select both your 16S rRNA gene sequence and genome sequence files as input. Click 'Execute' and your job will be put in the queue system so get a coffee and wait for the results!

Due to the mass of analysis which is conducted by Protologger, once your job is running, we expect it to take 3-5 hours for completion



What are protologues and why are they needed?

According to the latest version of the International Code on the Nomenclature of Prokaryotes (ICNP), publication of a novel taxon must include a description of its features. The format of this information is termed a 'protologue', of which some examples are provided below. Whilst the ICNP are vague on the specifics of what should be included, generally protologues include: the functional features, strain designation, isolation source and taxonomic placement of the species in relation to existing validly named taxa.
Therefore, Protologger provides all the necessary information for writing protologues, reducing the burden on cultivation experts for the validation of names for novel taxa.

Known issues

-Weird similarity scores; This can be caused by having a 16S rRNA sequence that contains none valid characters such as '-', which are taken into account during calculation, but are meaningless. To solve this please remove any such characters from your sequence and then re-upload and run Protologger again.

-Protologger fails when running GTDB-TK; This has been noticed to occur for highly contaminated genomes (>100%). If this occurs please check the quality data provided by Protologger via checkM, if your genome is contaminated we recommend re-sequencing of the pure culture.




If you have any issues regarding using Protologger please contact the authors of the original paper.
