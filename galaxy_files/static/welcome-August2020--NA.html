What is Protologger?

This includes providing;

Taxonomic placement
- 16S rRNA identity values
- 16S rRNA gene phylogenetic tree
- Taxonomic assignment based on GTDB (GTDB-TK r89)
- Genomic tree (phylophlan3)
- ANI values (FastANI)
- POCP values
Functional analysis
- KEGG based pathway reconstruction
- CAZyme profiling
- Antibiotic resistance profiling (CARD)
Ecological analysis
- Prevelance and average relative abundance across 1,000 samples for 19 unique environments (IMNGS)
- Occurance based on MASH comparison to a database of >50,000 MAGs from thousands of samples








What are protologues and why are they needed?
According to the latest version of the International Code on the Nomenclature of Prokaryotes (ICNP), publication of a novel taxa must include a description of that taxas features. The format of this information is termed a 'protologue', of which some examples are provided on the example page. Whilst the ICNP are vague on the specificis of what should be included, generally protologues include; the functional features, isolation source and taxonomic placement of the species in relation to existing validly named taxa.
Therefore, Protologger provides all the nessecary information for writing protologues, reducing the burden on cultivation experts for the validation of names for novel taxa.







Instructions
To use this Galaxy instance we suggest first registering an account (GDPR mode has been activate in compliance with European law) which will provide you with 10Gb of space, suitable for multiple analysis.
For the sake of simplicity in your analysis, we suggest you next make individualised 'Histories' for each isolate you run through Protologger. To do this click on the '+' on the left next to 'History'. You can then name the history based on the isolate you are currently studying by clicking on 'Unnamed history' and editing it as needed..
The first thing you need to do in your isolate specific history is upload your data! To do this you can click 'Get data' on the tool bar to the right and upload your genome and 16S rRNA sequence from your PC.
Now you are ready to run Protologger so click on the 'Protologer' tab under the 'Tools' bar on the right and click again on Protologger. This will bring you to the input selection screen where you can select both your 16S rRNA gene sequence and genome sequence files as input. Click 'Execute' and your job will be put in the queue system so get a coffee and wait for the results!
Due to the mass of analysis which is conducted by Protologger, once your job is running, we expect it to take ~3 hours till completion




If you have any issues regarding using Protologger please contact the authors of the original paper.
